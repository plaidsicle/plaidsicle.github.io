Bad Ideas About Writing,
Chapter by Chapter

This site hosts individual chapters of Cheryl Ball and Drew Loewe's (2017) open-access collection Bad Ideas About Writing alongside their matching episodes from Kyle Stedman's (2020-2022) audio version. The original text of Bad Ideas About Writing is available as a PDF textbook via West Virginia University Libraries. The podcast adaptation is available via Spotify (once upon a time, Anchor.fm) and in most other podcatchers. The book, the podcast, and this website are all licensed with a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license.
